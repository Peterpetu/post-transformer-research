Sixteen architectures, one standard, no winners.

Twenty months of attention-free, backprop-free and grown language models, audited by one rule: a number counts only if it traces to a run and a fair control. Three real models survived. All three lose to a same-size transformer. The useful part is why the rest never had a chance.

The six that survived the standard, in the order they were built. Blue is the part under test; grey is the standard machinery around it.

What survived

Six programmes with a measured number, a control built to be fair, and a public repository. Left, the mechanism. Right, the measurement that decided it.

DPNCA-LM

3.512 vs 3.346validation cross-entropy in nats, against a same-size GPT-2 at the same token budget

Attention replaced by six steps of a causal cellular automaton. 0.083% of the parameters do all the cross-position work, so reach grows additively and stops at 433 of 1,024 tokens. 158.9M parameters, one run on 250M Pile tokens, weights released.

Code, training log and weights

NCALM

29 of 30checkpoints at which the best local-rule variant trailed the GPT-2 baseline

Fifteen local update rules against a GPT-2 trained in the same harness with the same tokens, tokenizer, seed and schedule. The closest variant kept four attention blocks and still lost. The ones with no attention landed 13 to 88 times behind.

Harness, baseline and every run

WaveLM

4 of 4ladder rungs lost to a byte transformer with matched degrees of freedom

A complex-valued byte model that is real, at 2.30 bits per byte on The Pile, and loses at every size. Ablations say what carries it: the pointwise nonlinearity, channel mixing and depth. The wave-specific parts add little.

Model, ladder and ablations

HDC-LM

0.998two-layer recall under this rule, where BPTT reaches 1.000 and chance is 0.016

A backprop-free rule for a phasor linear-attention model, controlled by the identical model trained with BPTT. Drop weight transport and nothing changes. Drop the reverse-time pass and recall collapses to chance. Time, not transport, is the wall.

Rule, experiments and tests

developmental-lm

2.86 vs 9.01cross-entropy at birth: a newborn decoded from a compact genome, against random

Six compact-genome programmes with kill criteria written before each run. The genomic bottleneck replicates on a 13M language model, with the embedding table exempt. Under environment shift, copying weights beats every compressed rebirth.

Protocols, registers and runs

FlyBrain-LLM

15 of 15seed pairs where the fly's sparse address beat Gated DeltaNet, and the verdict under the rule frozen before the run: falsified

Two fly-derived mechanisms, each pre-registered with a hash-locked protocol and the full control set. The sparse address removes a capacity limit, but so does any bigger store, and it is slower on a GPU. The history states' small module-level gain vanished in a trained model. No fly mechanism earned the language-model test.

Sixteen times, the new mechanism never reached a gradient

Implemented, wired in, logged, and structurally unable to influence learning. Not noticed at the time, in any of the sixteen. Each would have been caught by a ten-minute ablation at the smallest scale that runs.

The new part owned almost none of the model, until it was made the model

in the programmerebuilt as the whole model

Where the mechanism reached a gradient at all, it was a sliver of the model it sat in, and the transformer around it did the work. Six of them were rebuilt with the mechanism as the load-bearing part; the next section measures what each one carries.

Backprop-free is a spectrum, and every point lost

Two-layer recall under each credit rule in HDC-LM, from the exact gradient to the most local. The closer to local, the worse.

The null arm, run

Each mechanism rebuilt as the whole model and trained beside its null arm (the mechanism switched off, everything else the same) and beside the incumbent it was meant to replace. Same bytes, same budget, same held-out score. Small by design: two seeds, two CPU cores.

the mechanism as the modelits null armthe incumbent

Every null arm sits on the bigram rung, and five of the six mechanisms sit far under it: they were inert in the programmes by wiring, not by nature. The cellular-automaton rule and the morphogen field, nearly the same model, are the only two that beat a tuned transformer at eight million bytes, and by twenty-four million the transformer has caught up. The three recurrences trail a trained GRU; the genome learns but never leaves the bigram rung.

The standard

Every statement in every programme got one of three grades. Only the third counts, and it is the only one drawn in blue on this page.

Claimed

It appears in prose. A result whose log no longer exists is claimed, whatever the document says.

Implemented

There is code, and it runs. A mechanism that provably cannot affect the output is implemented, however often it was logged.

Measured

A number in a log, traceable to the run that produced it, against a baseline built to be fair.

Read

57 branch refs across 17 repositories, every claim graded, fifteen facets scored. Nothing checked out, modified or built.

Attack

The four claims that would have anchored the top of the ranking. All four at least partially refuted, two of them the audit's own.

Draw

Each implemented architecture drawn from source, with the novel parts and the provably inert paths marked.

Verify

Every drawing attacked line by line: 851 of 904 citations verified, every parameter count recomputed, two corrected.

Before scaling any new mechanism, ablate it at the smallest scale that runs.

I diagnosed most of these failures in writing at the time. What I did instead of ablating was start the next programme. The two programmes since the audit are built around this habit.

Repositories

Fresh history, every number traceable to a file. The twelve audited programmes without a repository stay private: nothing in them meets the standard.
